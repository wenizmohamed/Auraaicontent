The Full Loop
How the five pieces connect into one self-improving workflow.
radix.synapseai-labs.com

1 · Brief

Feed in the client's objective, pillars & channels — once.

2 · Generate

AI drafts a month of ideas, then images & video in the Creative Tool.

3 · Review

Regenerate, edit or approve each piece — humans stay in control.

4 · Schedule

Drop approved content on the calendar, publish to every channel.

5 · Learn

Every approval + edit feeds the knowledge base. Next month is sharper.

It's a loop, not a line. The "Learn" step feeds back into "Brief" — Radix gets more accurate to each client's voice the more the team uses it. Multi-AI fallback keeps it running even when one provider has an outage.
