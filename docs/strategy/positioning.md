# RadixContent — Positioning

## One-line position
**RadixContent is the bilingual AR/EN content calendar OS for Gulf-region marketing agencies.**

## Why this wedge
1. **Existing demo data is Gulf-themed.** The Al Fardan Exchange seed (UAE remittance, Ramadan/Eid moments, Emirati Women's Day) maps directly to the buyer.
2. **Founder-market fit.** Weni knows the GCC agency landscape — language, holidays, client expectations, WhatsApp-first communication norms.
3. **Weakest competitor density.** Jasper, Buffer, Later, ContentStudio, Hootsuite all treat Arabic as an afterthought (no dialect awareness, no Hijri calendar, no WhatsApp deep-link export). The category leaders are competing in EN; AR is wide open.
4. **Defensible moat.** Three concrete things English-first tools cannot copy in a quarter:
   - Dialect-aware generation (MSA / Khaleeji / Egyptian / Levantine prompt variants with eval harness)
   - Hijri + Gregorian dual-calendar with Ramadan-tone presets and per-country GCC national days
   - WhatsApp Business export (the channel Gulf agencies actually use to get client approval)
5. **Buyer concentration.** UAE alone has ~3,000 marketing/PR agencies in DMCC/free zones; KSA Vision-2030 marketing spend is mandated to keep accelerating; Qatar/Bahrain/Oman/Kuwait round out a 30M-population buyer pool with high willingness to pay.

## Who we are NOT for (sharpens the wedge)
- Solo creators (use Buffer/Later)
- Pure brand teams (use Sprout/Hootsuite)
- US/EU agencies who don't run AR campaigns
- Anyone who needs publishing/scheduling (we plan, we don't post)

## Buyer titles
- Agency founder / Managing Director (1–15 people shops)
- Head of Content / Strategy
- Account Director (the human who babysits the calendar today)

## Pain we eliminate
- 8–12 hours/month per client spent in PowerPoint/Notion building calendars
- Translation round-trips and copy-paste errors between EN and AR drafts
- Missing Ramadan/Eid timing windows because nobody mapped Hijri to Gregorian
- Client approval friction (PDF over WhatsApp vs. email-attachment tennis)

## Proof points to gather (Phase 5)
- Hours saved per calendar (time on tool before vs. after) — target ≥ 6 hrs/month/client
- Approval cycle time (brief → approved calendar) — target < 48h
- Generated-post acceptance rate (kept-as-is vs. rewritten) — target ≥ 70%
