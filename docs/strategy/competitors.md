## Where each competitor wins
- **Jasper:** brand voice memory, marketplace of templates, enterprise IAM. Strong English. We don't compete on EN-first features.
- **Buffer / Later:** publishing + scheduling + analytics. We don't publish. Pitch as complementary.
- **ContentStudio:** content discovery + automation. Generic AI. No GCC hooks.
- **Hootsuite:** enterprise compliance, large-team workflows. Bloat for 5-person agencies.
