# Target list — 30 GCC agencies (Phase 5 outreach)

**Goal:** 5 design partners on free 30-day pilots by end of Week 10. 3 convert to paid by Week 12.

**Format:** fill in via founder-led research. Do NOT outsource. The research IS the qualifier.

## Target profile
- 1–15 employees
- Active on LinkedIn or Instagram (proves they care about content)
- ≥ 3 active retained clients (proves they need a multi-client tool)
- HQ in UAE / KSA / Qatar / Bahrain / Kuwait / Oman
- Bilingual AR/EN service offering on their website
- Not a Jasper/HubSpot reseller (will defend incumbent)

## Outreach sequence (per agency)
1. **Day 0** — LinkedIn connection request, 1-line note: "Saw your campaign for [client]. Built a tool for Gulf agencies — would love your read on it. No pitch, 15 min."
2. **Day 3** — Email if no LI response. Subject: "[Their agency name] + Hijri-aware calendar tool". Body: 4 sentences, link to demo loom.
3. **Day 7** — Reply to original thread with a free pilot offer (30 days, no card).
4. **Day 14** — One-line "still relevant?" ping.
5. **Day 21** — Mark dead. Note reason in `status` column.

## Seed sources for finding targets
- LinkedIn: search "Marketing Agency Founder UAE", "Content Director Dubai", filter by 1st/2nd-degree
- Dubai Lynx 2025 attendee list (paid agencies that compete on creative)
- DMCC Free Zone marketing-services member directory
- "Top 50 MENA Agencies" Campaign ME annual report
- Instagram: agencies tagging multiple GCC F&B / hospitality / fintech clients in their portfolio
- The Al Fardan Exchange agency-of-record (warm intro via Weni)

## Status legend
- `not contacted` — in queue
- `messaged` — outreach sent, waiting
- `replied-interested` — booked discovery call
- `pilot-active` — on free trial
- `paid` — converted to a paying plan
- `dead-no-fit` — said no, with logged reason
- `dead-no-reply` — 3 touches, no response
