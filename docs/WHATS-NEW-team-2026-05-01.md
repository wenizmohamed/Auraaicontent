# What's new in Radix this week

A quick tour of the upgrades. **Hard-refresh** the page once
(Cmd+Shift+R on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows) and you'll see them.

---

## You'll notice these right away

**Smarter chat dock**
Click the little Radix bot in the bottom-right corner. The "thinking"
animation now shimmers through stages ("Pulling context", "Generating",
"Almost there"). Hover any reply to copy or retry it. Three new
one-click assistants: **Brand Q&A**, **Extract from Text**, and
**Research → Write**.

**Calendar that actually feels like a calendar**
Open `/calendar`. There's a new rail on the right showing every
unscheduled draft — drag any of them onto a day to schedule it.
Months now slide in and out instead of jumping. Each day shows tiny
platform labels instead of plain dots. Hover a day with posts to
preview the captions.

**Brand-new Agent page**
Go to `/agent`. Two tabs:
- **Plan** — preview what the assistant is about to do, step by step,
  then approve or cancel.
- **History** — replay any past assistant run, in order, with
  everything it did.

**Refreshed dashboard**
The AI section now shows a live orbiting diagram of which AI is
handling each request, with a quick "Why this matters" explainer
beside it.

**Cleaner sign-in screen**
Form on the left, brand panel on the right ("Generate. Schedule.
Ship.") with a quiet paper texture.

**Cleaner pricing page**
Three tiers at `/settings/billing`. The recommended plan has a soft
highlight; the others are calm hairlines.

**Subtle upgrade banner**
A small one-line nudge sits at the top of the page if your workspace
is on the free plan. Vanishes once you upgrade. You can also dismiss
it for the session.

**AI quick-actions on any text**
Highlight any text in a brief or post → a small toolbar pops up.
Click **More** for 17 quick actions: translate to Arabic / French /
Spanish / English, make it more professional / friendly / playful,
summarize, expand, extract entities or action items, and more.

---

## Behind the scenes

- **AI spending safety net** — every workspace now has a monthly AI
  spending limit (default: unlimited). Once you set one, the system
  refuses AI calls before they go over budget.
- **Faster everywhere** — pages load only when you visit them. First
  screen is well under the speed target.
- **Three themes** — Dark (default), Light, and Studio (high-contrast,
  great for screenshots and screen sharing). Press **Cmd+Shift+L** to
  cycle.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** — press **?** anywhere to see them all.
  **⌘K** opens the global search-and-jump bar.
